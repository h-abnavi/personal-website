About me

I am a PhD researcher in power semiconductor devices at the University of Twente. My work focuses on developing and optimizing advanced device concepts for power switching applications.

I have experience throughout the device-development cycle, including physics-based design, device and process simulation, circuit simulation, fabrication, electrical characterization, parameter extraction, and benchmarking.

Selected research

Silicon power devices

Accumulation-mode trench bidirectional switch

Developed a novel RESURF accumulation-mode trench device architecture for efficient bidirectional power switching. The work resulted in a first-author IEEE journal publication.

TCAD · RESURF · Bidirectional switching

Silicon carbide

SiC trench AccuFET

Designed and evaluated an accumulation-mode SiC trench MOSFET concept for high-voltage applications, focusing on conduction performance and device scaling.

SiC · Trench MOSFET · High voltage
