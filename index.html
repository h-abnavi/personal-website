About me

I am a PhD researcher in power semiconductor devices at the University of Twente. My research focuses on the design and engineering of novel accumulation-mode MOSFETs and bidirectional power switches for energy-efficient power-electronics applications, such as battery chargers and battery protection.

My experience covers physics-based device design, process and device simulation, circuit simulation, electrical characterisation of fabricated devices, parameter extraction, performance optimization, and benchmarking against industrial reference devices.

My current work includes vertical trench MOSFETs, bidirectional power switches, dielectric RESURF concepts, nanosheet power FETs, and electrostatic scaling of advanced semiconductor architectures.

Selected research

Accumulation-mode trench bidirectional switch

Silicon carbide

SiC trench AccuFET

Extended the accumulation-mode concept to a SiC trench MOSFET for high-voltage applications, with emphasis on conduction performance, channel resistance, breakdown voltage, and device scaling. Oral presentation at ISPS 2025.
